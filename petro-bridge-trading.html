How a deal works

Six steps from first message to completed delivery, with documents at each stage.

  1. Inquiry

    Tell us the product, quantity, destination and delivery window.

  2. Offer

    We send a priced offer with specification and delivery terms.

  3. Verification

    Both sides complete company checks and sanctions screening.

  4. Contract

    We agree the sales contract and the payment instrument.

  5. Shipment

    Independent inspection at loading, then cargo documents are issued.

  6. Completion

    Payment is settled against documents once discharge is confirmed.
